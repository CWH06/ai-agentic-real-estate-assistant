# California Real Estate Information Boundaries

Document ID: `california-real-estate`

Last reviewed: 2026-08-14

Purpose: Give the assistant a cautious, official-source-oriented framework for general California homebuying questions.

Important: This document is educational, not legal, tax, lending, inspection, or brokerage advice. Requirements can depend on the property and transaction and can change. Users should verify current California Department of Real Estate materials and consult an appropriately licensed professional when a decision has legal or financial consequences.

## Disclosures and Transaction Documents

California residential transactions can involve multiple disclosures and reports. California Department of Real Estate consumer materials identify examples such as the Real Estate Transfer Disclosure Statement and the Disclosure Regarding Real Estate Agency Relationship. Other documents may be required or appropriate depending on the property, location, representation, financing, and transaction.

The assistant must not treat a short list as a complete disclosure checklist. It may explain the general purpose of a document when the indexed source supports that explanation, but it should direct users to current DRE materials or professional advice for applicability, deadlines, completion, or legal consequences.

## Agency Relationships

An agency disclosure helps parties understand whom real estate agents represent in a transaction. The assistant should not infer a particular agent's duties, conflicts, or legal relationship from MLS fields alone. Questions about a specific agreement should be answered by reviewing that agreement with the broker or qualified counsel.

## Property and Listing Verification

MLS data may be delayed, incomplete, changed, or supplied by different sources. Users should independently verify availability, price, boundaries, permits, square footage, school assignments, association information, and material property facts before relying on them.

School district mapping in this project is a geographic data join. A mapped district is useful for search but is not a guarantee of school attendance eligibility. District boundaries, grade-level assignments, school capacity, and enrollment rules should be confirmed with the relevant school district.

## Fair and Careful Answers

The assistant should answer factual property and market questions without steering users based on protected characteristics or making claims about who belongs in a neighborhood. For subjective location questions, it should offer objective, user-selected criteria such as commute, price, property features, or distance to services.

The assistant should identify uncertainty instead of inventing missing facts. When the indexed context does not support an answer, it should say that it lacks enough information.

## Current-Source Rule

California Department of Real Estate publications state that real estate law changes. For current legal requirements, use the latest official DRE law book, consumer pages, forms, and publications rather than relying only on this snapshot.
